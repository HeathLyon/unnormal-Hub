A collection of permitted Reddit stories alongside an original investigation into a reportedly haunted schoolhouse and the unsettling image of three red eyes.

FINDINGS
- The episode mixes retold personal accounts with the hosts’ own field investigation.
- Eyewitness reports preserve what a person experienced, but they do not by themselves identify the cause.
- Location history, lighting, sound, timestamps, and independent witnesses are especially valuable when reviewing a haunting claim.
