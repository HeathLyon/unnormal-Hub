SUMMARY
Jace and Ben read listener stories centered on strange waking experiences, disrupted identity, dreams, and the unnerving feeling of being in the wrong body.

FINDINGS
- Experiences near sleep and waking can feel vivid while memory, perception, and bodily awareness are still shifting.
- Sleep paralysis, false awakenings, vivid dreams, and dissociation provide possible frameworks but should not be assumed to explain every story.
- Firsthand accounts are most useful when the witness records them quickly and separates remembered details from later interpretation.
