SUMMARY
The episode follows the Vril Society legend from Edward Bulwer-Lytton’s fictional subterranean civilization into occultism, Nazi-era mythology, and modern conspiracy culture.

FINDINGS
- “Vril” originated as a fictional energy in the 1871 novel The Coming Race.
- Stories of a powerful prewar Vril Society expanded substantially after World War II and often blend fiction, occult history, and unsupported claims.
- The documented history of Nazi ideology and violence should be distinguished from later sensational stories about secret supernatural technology.
