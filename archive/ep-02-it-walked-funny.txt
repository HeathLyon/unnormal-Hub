SUMMARY
A collection of firsthand strange-encounter stories shared with permission, including sightings made unsettling by the way an unknown figure moved.

FINDINGS
- These stories are personal accounts and should be treated as testimony rather than independently verified evidence.
- Darkness, distance, stress, and unfamiliar movement can make an ordinary figure appear profoundly strange.
- Repeated details across stories can be useful for comparison, but similarity alone does not establish a shared cause.
