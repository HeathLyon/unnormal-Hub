SUMMARY
Jace and Ben examine listener-submitted true stories, including a strange light observed circling in a way the witness could not readily explain.

FINDINGS
- A careful light-sighting report benefits from time, direction, elevation, weather, movement, duration, and nearby aircraft information.
- Describing what a light did is more reliable than immediately assigning it an identity.
- Multiple independent witnesses or synchronized recordings can make later comparison much stronger.
