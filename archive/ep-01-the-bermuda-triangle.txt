SUMMARY
The first Unnormal Stories episode explores famous incidents and theories associated with the Bermuda Triangle, the loosely defined region between Florida, Bermuda, and Puerto Rico.

FINDINGS
- The Bermuda Triangle is a popular name rather than an officially recognized geographic region.
- Weather, navigation errors, mechanical failures, and the Gulf Stream can complicate accidents and searches in this heavily traveled area.
- Later retellings sometimes omit ordinary explanations or repeat details that are difficult to verify.
